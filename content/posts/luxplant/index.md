New post, new lamp...

But this one is a bit different as it is more of a kit. Everything is designed by Luxplant, and the 3D models, BOM and tutorial to build it are available on his patreon [here](https://www.patreon.com/luxplant/posts/luxplant-mini-165618629).

The price is quite low (less than 10$) and the quality of the 3D print models is really great, everything clicks perfectly. I will not share pictures of the build, as everything is available on his patreon, but here are some beauty shots of the final lamp. This one is the Luxplant Mini, and he is currently finalizing a Standard model way bigger than this one.
